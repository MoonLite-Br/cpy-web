Limits

Where cpy intentionally differs from CPython.

Areacpy
int64-bit only; no bigint
BytesNo bytes / bytearray
NumbersNo complex
SyntaxNo match / case
ObjectsNo __slots__; limited metaclasses
ConcurrencyCooperative async only
Large CPython ecosystems (native extensions, full stdlib, packaging tools) are out of scope.

Good fit

  • Small tools as one binary
  • Termux / limited environments
  • Reading a small interpreter codebase
